Privacy & Analytics

Visit statistics

When connected, this portfolio uses Umami Cloud to understand visits: time, pages viewed, referring website, approximate location, and browser/device information. The site does not ask the analytics service to collect visitors' names or email addresses, and does not use analytics cookies or session recordings.

Statistics are intended for the site owner's private Umami account and are processed by Umami Cloud. They do not identify a particular professor or confirm a visitor's university. Browser privacy signals and the exclusion setting below are respected.

URL query parameters and fragments are removed from the visit data. Only the referring website's origin is retained. No visits before activation can be recovered.
