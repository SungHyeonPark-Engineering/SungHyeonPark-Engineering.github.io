Modified-binder preparation

Purpose
Mix and disperse a modifier in a base binder, then assess the prepared material through separate property tests.
Measurements & units
Input mass (g or kg), content with a defined mass basis (%), temperature (°C), time and rotation rate (rpm).
Interpretation limits
The modifier, temperature, duration, and improvement in performance cannot be inferred from the preparation photograph.

Aggregate grading and sieving

Purpose
Characterize particle-size distribution and prepare the aggregate skeleton of a mixture.
Measurements & units
Sieve size (mm or µm), retained mass (g), cumulative retained and passing mass (%).
Interpretation limits
Photographs of aggregate do not establish a complete grading curve or compliance.

Binder components and transfer

Purpose
Prepare binder components and record their weighing and transfer before mixture or beam preparation.
Measurements & units
Component mass (g or kg), defined mixing ratio, temperature (°C), and elapsed time.
Interpretation limits
Appearance does not identify chemical components, suppliers, or the meaning of sample abbreviations.

Weighing and portioning

Purpose
Reproduce material quantities and prepare portions for different tests.
Measurements & units
Container, material, and mixture mass (g or kg), with tare and net/gross status.
Interpretation limits
A single balance reading does not establish binder content, density, or specimen identity.

Measurements & units
Mass, defined binder-content basis (%), mixing temperature (°C), time, and separately measured volumetric properties.
Interpretation limits
Different photographs may show different materials and batches. No confidential recipe is published.

Compaction and specimen dimensions

Purpose
Form specimens for subsequent density and mechanical tests, then measure their geometry.
Measurements & units
Mass (g), diameter and height (mm), temperature (°C), and documented compaction history.
Interpretation limits
A compactor prepares a specimen; it is distinct from a Marshall loading frame. Standard dimensions are not assigned to photographs without records.

Marshall stability and flow

Purpose
Evaluate load resistance and associated deformation of compacted mixtures under defined conditions.
Measurements & units
Stability (N or kN), flow with its recorded units, specimen dimensions, temperature, and loading rate.
Interpretation limits
Interpret stability and flow together with volumetric properties. Strength alone does not establish fatigue or cracking resistance.

Cantabro loss

Purpose
Evaluate particle loss from compacted mixture specimens exposed to repeated impact and abrasion in a drum.
Measurements & units
Pre-test and post-test specimen mass (g); loss = (initial − remaining mass) / initial mass × 100 (%).
Interpretation limits
Compare specimens under equivalent temperature, void, and rotation conditions. Separate images are not matched mass records.

Indirect tensile strength

Purpose
Apply diametral compression to a cylindrical specimen to compare resistance to tensile failure.
Measurements & units
Maximum load (N or kN), diameter and thickness (mm), and strength (MPa).
Interpretation limits
A single strength result does not provide TSR; conditioned and unconditioned records are required. The existing project page provides the previously published test image.

Maximum theoretical specific gravity

Purpose
Remove interparticle air from loose mixture to establish a reference for volumetric evaluation.
Measurements & units
Gmm (dimensionless), density with units, mass (g), water temperature (°C), and recorded de-airing conditions.
Interpretation limits
This is distinct from submerged weighing of a sealed compacted specimen. Density and specific gravity have different units.

Gmb (dimensionless), density (g/cm³ or kg/m³), air/submerged masses, and water temperature.

Bag integrity and correction information require records. The photograph does not establish a device brand or measurement validity.

Unsealed submerged weighing

Purpose
Use specimen mass and water displacement for bulk-density evaluation under the applicable method.
Measurements & units
Mass (g), bulk specific gravity (dimensionless), density, and water temperature.
Interpretation limits
Absorption and drainage affect weighing. Wiping alone does not prove saturated-surface-dry condition or standard compliance.

Lueer fluidity

Purpose
Assess the workability of heated mastic asphalt through timed movement of the test penetrator.
Measurements & units
Fluidity time (s), mixture temperature (°C), and documented mixing history.
Interpretation limits
Fluidity time is not viscosity in Pa·s. A shorter time is not universally preferable.

Wheel tracking and dynamic stability

Purpose
Assess permanent deformation under repeated wheel loading.
Measurements & units
Rut depth (mm), wheel passes, dynamic stability (passes/mm), temperature, and load.
Interpretation limits
Interpret the deformation curve and stated calculation interval. Ordinary rutting tests and Mode II simulation are different procedures.

Mode II shear/reflection-cracking simulation

Purpose
Observe cracking in a composite pavement specimen under simulated joint movement and wheel loading.
Measurements & units
Documented cycle count and any measured displacement or crack dimensions; qualitative damage observations.
Interpretation limits
This project-specific simulation is not presented as a standardized pure Mode II fracture-toughness test. Internal result labels are withheld.

Texas Overlay preparation and testing

Purpose
Simulate repeated opening and closing across an underlying discontinuity to study reflection cracking.
Measurements & units
Cycle count, force, displacement, and temperature; only metrics actually calculated by the applied procedure.
Interpretation limits
Preparation, mounted specimens, and damage observations are distinct records. Reaching a test limit is not an observed failure at that limit.

Falling Weight Deflectometer

Purpose
Evaluate pavement structural response non-destructively using load and a deflection basin.
Measurements & units
Load (kN), deflection (µm or mm), sensor spacing, and temperature.
Interpretation limits
Layer moduli are model-based backcalculations. A trailer photograph does not establish a loading event or a diagnostic result.

British Pendulum Test

Purpose
Compare surface-friction response using a pendulum apparatus under defined surface and slider conditions.
Measurements & units
BPN (dimensionless), repeated readings, location, and temperature.
Interpretation limits
BPN is not interchangeable with a vehicle friction coefficient, Skid Number, or braking distance.

Field permeability

Purpose
Observe water-level change or flow through pavement using an identified apparatus and method.
Measurements & units
Elapsed time (s), head/level, volume, and method-specific infiltration or permeability measures.
Interpretation limits
Surface water alone does not prove leakage. Timing, seal checks, and flow-path records are needed; the held water-trace photographs remain unpublished.
