Low-temperature binder performance

Is −20°C an appropriate test condition for high-viscosity asphalt binders?

Research objective

This study examined the low-temperature flexural behavior of high-viscosity asphalt binders and the suitability of the −20°C test condition. Laboratory observations were examined alongside statistical analysis.

My contribution

  • Prepared high-viscosity binder specimens and supported low-temperature three-point bending tests under Korean Industrial Standard (KS) F 2491.
  • Assisted with statistical analysis and manuscript preparation.
  • Checked laboratory records against analysis datasets and reported results.

The study’s three-point bending test

This study used low-temperature three-point bending of molded high-viscosity asphalt binder beams under KS F 2491 (2017). The loading head moved at a prescribed rate at midspan, and the load–time response was analyzed to examine the −20 °C test condition. The study used molded binder beams measuring 120 × 20 × 20 mm.

BBR · Bending Beam Rheometer

BBR also bends a binder beam, but holds a small constant load and measures deflection over time to determine flexural creep stiffness S(t) and the m-value. The loading protocol and measured response differ from this study's flexural test.
