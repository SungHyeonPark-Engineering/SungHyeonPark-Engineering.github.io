How do bridge-deck materials respond to repeated displacement?

Godeok–Topyeong Bridge and pavement-material evaluation

Godeok–Topyeong Bridge is a two-pylon concrete cable-stayed bridge across the Han River, connecting Godeok-dong in Gangdong-gu, Seoul, with Topyeong-dong in Guri. This page documents my specimen preparation, Texas Overlay testing, observation and analysis, and reporting for bridge-deck pavement-material evaluation.

When it opened in 2025, Hyundai E&C described it as the world’s longest-span concrete cable-stayed bridge, with a 540 m main span between its two pylons.

The bridge is part of Section 14 of the Anseong–Guri segment of the Sejong–Pocheon Expressway, implemented by Korea Expressway Corporation and constructed by Hyundai E&C.

Research objective

This evaluation examined the cracking behavior of mastic asphalt bridge-deck pavement materials under repeated displacement. It compared an LMC reference with mastic asphalt from three suppliers. My role covered laboratory evaluation of pavement materials and documentation of the results.

My contribution

  • Prepared latex-modified concrete (LMC) reference specimens and mastic asphalt specimens from three suppliers.
  • Performed Texas Department of Transportation (TxDOT) Overlay testing.
  • Analyzed load-versus-cycle responses and observed crack progression and documented the findings in a technical report.
