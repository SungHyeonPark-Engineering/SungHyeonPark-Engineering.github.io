Complementary research · Engineering practice

Ground-anchor experimental validation

Complementary engineering experience in full-scale testing, instrumentation records, and load-transfer interpretation.

My contribution

I participate in staged loading tests of full-scale high-inclination ground anchors and monitor loads and displacements. I check instrumentation records for consistency and traceability, and contribute to the interpretation of load-transfer behavior and finite element analysis results and to technical reporting.
