Selected pavement research

Research experience

Research interests: Asphalt mixture design and performance evaluation; mechanistic modeling; low-temperature asphalt binder behavior; laboratory cracking tests; porous pavement field performance; carbon-free asphalt technology.

01 2023

Godeok Topyeong Bridge pavement evaluation

Commissioned by Hyundai Engineering & Construction
Research organization: Korean Society of Civil Engineers

Performed TxDOT Overlay tests on three mastic asphalt mixture types: TLA-based, polymer-modified, and polymer-modified mixtures for ultra-thin layers.

Analyzed load–cycle data and test-stage photographs; documented crack progression in a technical report. Tested pre-prepared latex-modified concrete (LMC) specimens.

Methods, contribution & evidence ↗

02 2023–2024

Porous asphalt field performance

Commissioned by Korea Expressway Corporation

Supported field permeability, skid-resistance, and falling weight deflectometer (FWD) measurements, data analysis, and technical reporting for porous asphalt pavements on the Jeongeup–Taein section of the Honam Expressway.

Methods, contribution & evidence ↗

03 2023–2024

Low-temperature performance of high-viscosity binders

Research organization: Korean Asphalt Institute

Prepared specimens, performed low-temperature flexural tests (to assess the suitability of the −20°C test condition under KS F 2491), and analyzed results.

Applied a two-sided t-test and attempted to fit published equations and estimate parameters; prepared monthly reports and assisted a master’s student with specimen and manuscript preparation.

Methods, contribution & evidence ↗

04 2024 (through Dec)

Epoxy asphalt mixture design and performance

Industry-linked research: USECHEM Co., Ltd.

Conducted aggregate sieve analysis, selected aggregate gradation, prepared mixture specimens, and performed Marshall stability, indirect tensile strength (ITS), Cantabro loss, and theoretical maximum specific gravity tests.

Analyzed results from these tests, as well as external resilient modulus and wheel-tracking tests, to evaluate epoxy asphalt mixture performance.

Methods, contribution & evidence ↗

05 2024

Anti-icing asphalt standard proposal

Standards body: Korean Agency for Technology and Standards (KATS)

Supported the principal investigator in preparing an anti-icing asphalt proposal for a Korean Industrial Standard, including background research, rationale, and supporting documents. The proposal reached final review; formal adoption remains incomplete.

Methods & capabilities

From specimens to field measurements

Laboratory testing

TxDOT Overlay, Marshall stability, ITS, Cantabro loss, and theoretical maximum specific gravity tests; aggregate sieve analysis, gradation selection, and mixture specimen preparation. Low-temperature three-point bending of high-viscosity asphalt binders (KS F 2491; displacement-controlled flexural testing, distinct from BBR creep testing).

Field measurement support

Permeability, skid resistance, and FWD testing; technical reporting and project documentation.

Analysis & engineering tools

Two-sided t-tests, attempts to fit published equations and estimate parameters, and data checks; AutoCAD drawings, SUNEX, and GeoStudio SLOPE/W for engineering support.
