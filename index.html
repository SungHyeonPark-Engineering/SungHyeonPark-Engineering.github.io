About

Academic Profile

I am a civil engineering graduate from Seoul National University of Science and Technology and a prospective Ph.D. applicant for Fall 2027. My primary research interest is pavement engineering, with a focus on asphalt materials, asphalt binder performance, recycled asphalt systems, and performance-based evaluation.

My research experience spans asphalt binder behavior, epoxy asphalt mixtures, recycled asphalt systems, field pavement performance monitoring, and pavement material standardization. In parallel, my professional engineering experience in geotechnical retaining systems has strengthened my ability to connect laboratory testing, field instrumentation, structural analysis, and performance-based infrastructure evaluation.

Education: B.S. in Civil Engineering, Seoul National University of Science and Technology, South Korea.

Selected Research Experience

Research and Validation Work

Pavement Materials

Asphalt Binder Low-Temperature Performance

Participated in undergraduate research on the low-temperature flexural behavior of high-viscosity asphalt binders. Responsibilities included laboratory assistance, experimental data organization, monthly research-note preparation, and manuscript editing for a journal publication.

Mixture Design

Epoxy Asphalt Mixtures and Performance Evaluation

Conducted mixture design and basic performance testing for epoxy asphalt mixtures through university and industry-linked research. Contributed to laboratory testing, report preparation, research documentation, and a journal paper on the engineering properties of domestic epoxy asphalt mixtures.

Field Evaluation

Porous Asphalt Pavement Field Performance Monitoring

Participated in a multi-year field performance monitoring project for a porous asphalt pavement test section. Contributed to field permeability testing, skid resistance testing, FWD-based evaluation, technical report preparation, presentation materials, and project documentation.

Standardization

Anti-Icing Asphalt Mixture Standardization

Contributed to a national standardization research project on anti-icing asphalt mixtures. Responsibilities included technical report preparation and support for drafting a proposed Korean Standard related to material and performance requirements.

Recycled Asphalt

Recycled Asphalt Binder and Rejuvenator Evaluation

Prepared research manuscripts on asphalt binder stiffness, crude oil composition, recycled asphalt binder performance, and the comparative effects of petroleum-based and bio-based rejuvenators. The work focused on physical properties, rheological behavior, and environmental performance perspectives.

Geotechnical Validation

High-Angle Ground Anchor and Retaining System Validation

As part of professional R&D work, participated in experimental validation of retaining systems using high-angle ground anchors. Contributed to anchor tensile testing, instrumentation planning, load-transfer mechanism analysis, displacement monitoring, technical report preparation, and conference poster development.
