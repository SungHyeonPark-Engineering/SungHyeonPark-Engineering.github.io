About

Academic Profile

Sung Hyeon Park is a civil engineering researcher preparing for Direct PhD applications in the United States. His primary research focus is pavement materials, asphalt binder performance, recycled asphalt systems, and experimental and analytical performance evaluation.

He earned a B.S. in Civil Engineering from Seoul National University of Science and Technology in South Korea. He currently works as an Associate Engineer in the Design Division of a Korean geotechnical engineering company, where his professional work connects geotechnical retaining systems, instrumentation-based testing, structural stability assessment, and technical documentation.
